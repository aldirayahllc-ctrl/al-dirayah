    03 — How it runs

    From survey to handover

    01Week 1

    Survey

    Site attendance, shaft and machine-room measurement, load and traffic assessment, and a written condition report on existing equipment.

    02Weeks 2–3

    Design & approvals

    Shop drawings, method statement and risk assessment, DMT and authority submissions, and OEM coordination where we are working under a principal.

    03Weeks 4–10

    Installation

    Rail alignment, machine set, car assembly and roping, executed under an OSHAD-compliant safety plan with daily permit control.

    04Week 11

    Test & commission

    Load testing, overspeed and brake verification, door timing, and third-party inspection witnessed to standard.

    05Ongoing

    Maintain

    Planned preventive visits on a fixed schedule, 24/7 callout, genuine spare parts held locally, and a written report after every attendance.
