    03 — How it runs

    From survey to handover

    01Week 1

    Survey

    Site attendance, shaft and machine-room measurement, load assessment, and condition reporting.

    02Weeks 2–3

    Design & approvals

    Shop drawings, method statement, DMT submissions, and OEM coordination under master subcontractor agreements.

    03Weeks 4–10

    Installation

    Rail alignment, machine setting, car assembly, and roping executed under an OSHAD-compliant plan.

    04Week 11

    Test & commission

    Load testing, overspeed brake verification, door timing, and third-party inspection to EN 81-20/50.

    05Ongoing

    Maintain

    Planned preventive visits, 24/7 callout, genuine spare parts held locally, and digital service logs.
