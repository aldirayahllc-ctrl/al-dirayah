03 — Continuous Circuit

Steps don't end.
They return.

Steps flatten, turn under the comb plate, and travel back along the underside of the truss on the same continuous chain. We inspect both carrying and return paths on every PM visit.

Carrying run: Steps under passenger load climbing at 30°.
Return run: Inverted return path beneath the truss where roller wear accumulates.

On a planned maintenance visit we lift comb plates and inspect step rollers, chain tension, and missing step safety switches to prevent unscheduled shutdowns.

05 — Safety Matrix

EN 115 Mandated Devices

Every safety circuit is tested and logged on every preventive service visit.

/ 01

Comb impact switch

Stops drive machine if objects get trapped between comb plate and step tread.

/ 02

Step sag sensor

Detects steps dropping below tread line prior to reaching the landing comb.

/ 03

Handrail entry guard

Halts operation if hands or clothing enter the handrail newel inlet.

/ 04

Step chain monitor

Monitors chain elongation to prevent rollback before brake engagement.

/ 05

Missing step detector

Halts travel immediately if a step is absent from the return run.

/ 06

Overspeed & reversal

Trips main safety circuit at >1.2× speed or unexpected direction reversal.

/ 07

Skirt switch

Detects entrapment between step sides and inner skirt panel.

/ 08

Emergency stop

Controlled-deceleration emergency push buttons at upper and lower landings.
