Residential Vertical Transport Solutions

Engineered for high-end luxury villas, duplex penthouses, and private estates in Abu Dhabi & Dubai.

01

MRL Gearless Villa Lifts

Ultra-quiet permanent magnet synchronous traction drive requiring zero dedicated machine room. Operates smoothly under 40 dB acoustic rating with low power consumption.

02

Low-Overhead Hydraulic Lifts

Ideal for retrofitting into existing residential structures. Requires only a shallow 200mm pit and minimal top floor overhead, featuring smooth hydraulic proportional valve leveling.

03

360° Panoramic Glass Cabins

Curved circular or polygonal laminated safety glass enclosures with champagne gold stainless steel structure. Creates a breathtaking architectural focal point in villa stairwells.

04

Pitless Pneumatic Vacuum Lifts

Air-driven self-supporting cylindrical elevators. Installs directly onto existing villa floor slabs without structural shaft excavation or pit drilling.

05

Custom Italian Interior Fit-Outs

Tailored interior finishes including backlit Calacatta marble flooring, hand-stitched leather wall accents, bronze mirror panels, and indirect ambient LED ceilings.

06

Automatic Emergency Rescuing

Equipped with automatic battery-powered Emergency Landing Device (ELD) that safely brings the cabin to the nearest floor and opens doors during power outages.
