Who We Are

Al Dirayah Electromechanical Contracting LLC is an approved subcontractor and engineering specialist across TKE, OTIS, and KONE vertical transport systems. We deliver end-to-end engineering, installation, testing, Civil Defence safety clearance, and 24/7 annual maintenance (AMC) across Abu Dhabi & Dubai.

Core Capabilities & Scope of Work

Vertical Transport

Lifts, escalators, and moving walkways — full turnkey installation, EN 81-20/50 compliance, safety upgrades, complete modernization, and scheduled AMC service.

Power & Electromechanical

Electromechanical plant installation, power distribution networks, electrical control panels, substations, and transmission line contracting.

Low Current / ELV Networks

CCTV surveillance systems, access control, structured cabling, and smart building monitoring under ADMCC & OSHAD compliance standards.

Supply & OEM Trading

Direct wholesale import of elevators, escalators, and 100% genuine factory OEM spare parts supporting project installations and maintenance fleets.
