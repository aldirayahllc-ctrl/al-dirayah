Engineering Consulting & Diagnostic Services

Independent engineering expertise to optimize building traffic, ensure safety compliance, and maximize lift lifecycle.

01

Traffic Analysis & Passenger Simulation

Computerized Monte Carlo passenger simulation adhering to CIBSE Guide D and ISO 22559 standards. Calculates optimal elevator quantity, speed, and handling capacity for commercial developments.

02

EN 81-20/50 Third-Party Safety Audits

Comprehensive third-party technical audit evaluating progressive safety gears, overspeed governors, brake torque, door lock contacts, and emergency illumination.

03

Structural Shaft Load Analysis & BIM

High-precision structural reaction load calculation (guide rail bracket reaction forces, pit impact forces) integrated into 3D BIM Revit CAD building models.

04

Abu Dhabi Civil Defence Recertification

Turnkey inspection management and official document submission for Abu Dhabi Civil Defence operational fire safety clearance certificates for vertical transport installations.

05

ISO 25745 Energy Efficiency Consulting

In-depth energy audit measuring standby and operational power consumption to assist developers in achieving Estidama Pearl and LEED Green Building certification.

06

Condition & Dilapidation Reports

Independent forensic assessment of aging elevator assets prior to building acquisition or major overhaul, providing detailed remaining useful life (RUL) estimates.
