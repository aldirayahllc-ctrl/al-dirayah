Engineered Accessibility Features

Every component is custom-tailored to provide effortless mobility, high contrast legibility, and maximum physical safety for senior citizens and People of Determination.

♿

EN 81-70 Wheelchair Accessibility

Extra-wide automatic telescopic sliding doors (800mm to 1100mm clear width) with zero-bump flush threshold sills, non-glare interior lighting, and durable anti-slip composite floor surfaces.

🎛️

Ergonomic Inclined COP & Braille

Dual-height Car Operating Panels (COP) positioned ergonomically at 900mm–1100mm height for seated wheelchair users, featuring raised tactile numbers and dual Arabic/English Braille characters.

🛗

Soft VVVF Acceleration Drive

Specialized low-jerk travel profiles engineered for gentle start, smooth deceleration, and ±2mm exact flush floor levelling to completely eliminate tripping hazards for seniors.

🚨

Automatic Floor Rescue & Voice

Integrated Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) lowers car to the nearest floor during power failures, automatically releases doors, and connects to 24/7 emergency dispatch hotline.

🪞

128-Beam Safety Curtain & Mirror

Full-height 128-beam infrared safety sensors instantly prevent door contact with passengers, paired with a rear wall glass mirror for safe wheelchair reversing visibility.

🛋️

Continuous Handrails & Folding Seat

Dual-tier 32mm brushed stainless steel grab rails extending across three cabin walls, with optional heavy-duty wall-mounted cushioned folding seat for senior passenger comfort.
